Features

Everything from the guide, organized as a product surface.

The extension combines a multi-AI side panel, webpage highlights, personal notes, highlight management, AI handoff actions, cookie banner blocking, and a toolbar badge.

AI side panel

Open AI services beside your current webpage from the toolbar or from selected text.

Service switching

Switch between ChatGPT, Gemini, Qwen, Claude, Kimi, Grok, DeepSeek, or your own custom URL.

Highlights

Select text on any website, then apply yellow, green, blue, pink, or orange highlights.

Notes

Add personal notes to highlighted text and edit them from the Highlights tab.

Highlight manager

Search, filter, copy, delete, and jump back to saved highlights from the side panel.

Cookie banner blocking

Automatically blocks supported OneTrust cookie consent banners with no setup required.

Privacy Policy

Clear data handling for AI Sidebar Hub + Highlights.

Version 1.0.0 - last updated April 7, 2026.

No advertising data use

AI Sidebar Hub + Highlights does not collect, sell, or share personal user data for advertising, profiling, or data resale.

Local browser storage

All highlights, notes, and settings are stored locally in the user's browser using Chrome storage. The extension does not store personal data on external servers.

User-selected text

The extension may process user-selected text to provide core features such as highlighting, note-taking, and AI-powered assistance.

No cross-site tracking

The extension does not track users across websites and does not collect browsing history beyond what is required to provide its core functionality.

AI features are explicit actions

When the user explicitly chooses to use AI features, such as Ask AI, selected text may be sent to the selected third-party AI service, including services such as OpenAI ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude. This data is transmitted only after user action and is not stored by the extension.

Troubleshooting

Fast checks for common issues.

The guide documents fallback paths for embedded AI pages, highlight matching, text handoff, and cookie banners.

AI not showing

Enable Strong embed, open the AI in a new tab, and confirm that you are logged in to the AI service.

Highlights missing after reload

Make sure you are on the exact same URL. If the page content changed, re-highlight the text.

Text not filling AI chat

Reload the AI page, try another AI service, and make sure the AI page is logged in and ready.

Cookie banner still showing

The extension only blocks OneTrust banners. Other consent providers are not covered.

Browser support

The extension is designed for Chrome. It may work on Edge, but Edge has not been tested.

Restore defaults

Use Defaults in settings to restore the original seven AI service entries.

FAQ

Answers from the product documentation.

These notes reflect the current version 1.0.0 documentation.

Are highlights synced across devices?

No. Highlights are currently saved only on your current browser. Sync functionality is planned for a future release.

Can I change the color of an existing highlight?

Currently, you need to delete the old highlight and create a new one with your preferred color.

Does the extension collect my personal data?

No. Highlights and notes are saved on your computer, and the extension does not collect or sell personal data.

Can I export highlights to a file?

File export is planned. For now, you can copy highlights to the clipboard and paste them into a text or Word document.

Does it work outside Chrome?

The extension is designed for Chrome. Chromium-based browsers may work, but they are not the tested target.
